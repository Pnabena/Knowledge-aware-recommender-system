The parrot logo and six restaurant category icons were supplied by the project owner as SVG artwork. The icons in `../icons/` are individual vector extracts from the supplied restaurant sheet: bakery, café, bar, pub, fine dining and fast food. Original paths and colours are retained; sheet headings and labels are omitted.

Category badges are decorative UI artwork and are never treated as business photos or visual model evidence. Interface/navigation icons continue to use Lucide.
